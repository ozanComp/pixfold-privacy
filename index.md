# Pixfold Privacy Policy

Pixfold processes the images you select on your device and saves JPEG copies in your shared photo library. Pixfold does not transmit these images to the developer. When you share an export, Android sends the JPEG copies to the app you choose. Images selected from a cloud-backed document provider are subject to that provider's privacy policy.

Pixfold stores your selected language and last known Pro entitlement on your device. Google Play handles payments and purchase restoration under its own privacy policy. Pixfold does not receive payment card details.

The free version uses Google AdMob to show a banner after successful export. Users can choose to complete a rewarded ad to unlock one batch of up to five photos. Google Mobile Ads may process IP addresses, device or account identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention. Where available, use Ad privacy choices in Pixfold to review or change your preferences. See [how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites). Pixfold Pro hides the result banner.

To remove local preferences, clear app storage or uninstall Pixfold. To remove exported images, delete them in your photo library.
